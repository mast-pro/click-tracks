How it lines up

The tempo is measured from the drum and note attacks in the audio. If your DAW wrote a BPM tag into the file, it is used as a guide, and you’ll see a note if the two disagree. You can always type the BPM yourself.

The count-in is timed so the song’s first sound lands exactly where the next click would be. The outro clicks follow a beat grid fitted to the last 20 seconds, so they sit on the ending even if the tempo drifts. If either end feels early or late, nudge it in milliseconds; the length of one beat is shown under the tempo.

Everything runs in your browser. The track never leaves your computer.
